## URL

The Management Hub is accessible at: [clicketflash.com/manage](https://www.clicketflash.com/manage/?mode=management)

## Overview

The Management Hub is the cloud-based brain of the ClickFlash ecosystem. It provides fleet-wide visibility, AI analytics, and remote support capabilities for all resort locations.

## Analytics & AI Forecasting

The dashboard features an **AI-Enhanced Analytics** engine that provides:

- **Revenue Predictions**: Weekly and monthly projected earnings based on recent photo volume and sales trends.
- **Strategic Insights**: AI-generated advice on staffing, promotion timing, and tier adjustments based on fleet performance.

## Fleet Monitoring

- **Station Health**: Real-time status (Online/Offline) of all Master stations.
- **Volume Metrics**: Total photos ingested and orders created across the entire resort network.
- **Sync Status**: Verification of cloud synchronization status for each site.

## Remote Support

- **Emergency Tunneling**: Admins can launch a remote terminal/desktop session to assist local Master station photographers directly from the Hub.
- **Global Settings Push**: Update pricing or configurations site-wide with a single click.

## Security

- **Strict Auth**: Requires JWT-based authentication.
- **Audit Logs**: Every administrative action is logged for security compliance.
