## Overview

The Master App is the central workstation for photographers. It handles photo ingestion, automated auditing, editing, and synchronization with the Touch kiosks.

## Getting Started

1. **Login**: Launch the application and enter your credentials.
2. **Importing Photos**:
   - Connect your SD card or camera via USB.
   - Click **Import** and select the source folder.
   - The system will automatically tier and audit your photos.

## AI-Enhanced Auditing

As you import, the Master App performs a local quality audit:

- **Dark Flag**: Indicates low exposure.
- **Overexposed Flag**: Indicates highlights are blown.
- **Flat Flag**: Indicates low contrast or poor lighting.

> [!TIP]
> Use these flags to prioritize which photos to edit or cull before they reach the customer kiosk.

## Editing Photos

1. Select an album from the **Albums** grid.
2. Click a thumbnail in the **Filmstrip** to load it into the editor.
3. Use the sidebar controls for:
   - **AI Retouch**: One-click professional enhancement.
   - **Crop & Rotate**: Standard framing adjustments.
   - **Filters**: Apply preset styles (e.g., B&W, Vivid).

## Kiosk Sync

Once editing is complete, click **Send to Kiosk**. This pushes finalized, watermarked assets to the Touch stations on the local network.

## Troubleshooting

- **Station Offline**: Ensure the Ethernet bridge is connected.
- **Import Failed**: Check available disk space (requires 100GB+ for large libraries).
