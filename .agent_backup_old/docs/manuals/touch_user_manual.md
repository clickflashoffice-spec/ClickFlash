## Overview

The Touch App is a guest-facing kiosk designed for autonomous photo selection and ordering. It operates entirely offline for maximum speed and security.

## Guest Workflow

### 1. Welcome Screen

Guests are greeted with a beautiful, immersive welcome screen. Simply tap **Find Your Photos** to begin.

### 2. Finding Photos

Guests can locate their photos using two methods:

- **Browse by Album**: Selecting the specific event or date.
- **Face Search**: Leveraging local AI to find photos containing their face (Strictly 100% offline).

### 3. Selection Grid

Guests tap photos to add them to their favorites or cart.

- High-quality previews are loaded instantly from the local Master station push.
- Guests can see itemized pricing as they select.

### 4. Order Finalization

1. Review the selection in the **Cart**.
2. Enter guest details (Name/Room #).
3. Confirm the order.
4. The order is pushed to the Master Station and synced to the Management Hub for fulfillment.

## Kiosk Administration

To access admin settings (e.g., pricing update, diagnostics):

1. Long-press the ClickFlash logo for 5 seconds.
2. Enter the **Admin Password** when prompted.
3. Use this mode to verify connection to the Master station.
