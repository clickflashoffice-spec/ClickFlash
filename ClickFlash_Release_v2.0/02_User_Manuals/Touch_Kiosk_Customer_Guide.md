# Touch Kiosk Customer Guide

## Customer Interaction Flow
1. **RFID / Wristband Scan**: Customers tap their resort wristband on the RFID reader or look at the camera for Face Vector recognition.
2. **Photo Grid**: Customers swipe through high-resolution preview photos.
3. **Cart & Checkout**: Selected photos are added to the local cart and synced instantly to the Master Portal.
4. **Admin Override**: Staff can trigger the admin diagnostic panel by pressing `Ctrl+Shift+Alt+F12`.
