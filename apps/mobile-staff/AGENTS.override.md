# Mobile Staff App Override Rules

> **Target**: `apps/mobile-staff` (Expo React Native, QR Scanner)

## Domain Context
Internal staff application used at physical locations for verifying customer credentials.

## Specific Constraints
- **Hardware**: QR ticket scanner functionality.
- **Offline Reliability**: Must support offline verification of QR codes/tickets if network connectivity drops.

## AI Instructions
Focus on rugged offline-first capabilities and fast camera barcode scanning response times.
