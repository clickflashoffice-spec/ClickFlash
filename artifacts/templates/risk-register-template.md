| Likelihood ↓ / Impact → | Low (1) | Medium (2) | High (3) | Very High (4) |
|-------------------------|---------|------------|----------|---------------|
| Very High (4) | Medium (4) | High (6) | High (8) | Critical (12) |

## Scoring Legend

### Likelihood

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Very High (4) | Almost certain to occur |
| High (3) | Likely to occur |
| Medium (2) | May occur |
| Low (1) | Unlikely to occur |

### Impact

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Very High (4) | Project failure, major breach |
| High (3) | Significant delay, major issue |
| Medium (2) | Moderate delay, manageable issue |
| Low (1) | Minimal impact |

## Risk Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| Open | Identified, no action taken |
| Mitigated | Controls in place, monitoring |
| Transferred | Ownership changed or insured |
| Accepted | Acknowledged, no further action |
| Closed | No longer applicable |
