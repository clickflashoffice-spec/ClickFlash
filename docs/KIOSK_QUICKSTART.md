# ClickFlash Kiosk Quickstart

## Find your photos in 5 easy steps

---

### 1️⃣ Enter your room number
[🔍]

**Type your room number** on the keypad and tap **Find My Photos**.

> 💡 Tip: Your room number is on your key card or booking confirmation.

---

### 2️⃣ Pick your photos
[👆]

Tap the photos you want to keep. A **green checkmark** means they are selected. Tap again to unselect.

---

### 3️⃣ Choose your products

| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| **Digital Download** — Every photo sent to your phone | $9.99 |
| **8×10 Print** — A glossy keepsake to take home | $14.99 |
| **Full Album** — All your photos, all downloads, and one print | $49.99 |

Tap the product you want, then tap **Next**.

---

### 4️⃣ Pay
[💳]

**Swipe, tap, or insert** your card when the light turns green. Follow the prompts on the card reader.

> ✅ **You are safe.** We never store your card number.

---

### 5️⃣ Get your receipt
[📄]

The screen shows your **order number** and a text message is sent to your phone.

> 📱 **Photos will be on your phone in 5 minutes.** We text you the link.

---

## 🆘 Need help?

**Press the red button** on the side of the screen. A staff member will be right with you.

---

*Thank you for choosing ClickFlash!* 📸
