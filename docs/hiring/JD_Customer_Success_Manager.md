# Job Description: Customer Success & Field Operations Manager (🔴 P0 Horizon 2)

> **Company:** ClickFlash (Autonomous Photography & Resort Media Platform)  
> **Location:** Remote (with 30-50% travel to resort venues)  
> **Compensation:** $90,000 - $120,000 base + performance bonus  
> **Equity:** 0.3% - 0.75% (Seed Stage, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)  
> **Reporting To:** CEO / Business Founder  

---

## About ClickFlash
ClickFlash is transforming resort photography into an effortless, high-revenue digital experience. We partner with luxury resorts, water parks, and attraction operators worldwide, providing automated camera stations, guest mobile passes, and AI-curated photo galleries.

---

## The Role
As our first Customer Success and Field Operations Manager, you will be the vital link between ClickFlash and resort management teams. You will oversee venue onboarding, train resort photographers and retail staff, monitor daily capture and sales performance, and guarantee extraordinary guest satisfaction.

---

## Key Responsibilities
- **Venue Launch & Onboarding:** Manage the operational rollout of ClickFlash at newly signed resort and hotel properties, ensuring staff are fully trained and motivated.
- **Photographer & Staff Training:** Create and deliver high-impact training workshops for on-site photographers on camera tethering, roaming capture tactics, and guest engagement.
- **Revenue & Performance Optimization:** Analyze daily venue metrics (capture volume, photo views, cart conversion rates) and collaborate with venue managers to maximize photo concession sales.
- **Operational Health & Support:** Act as the direct point of contact for resort General Managers and operations directors, resolving day-to-day operational issues swiftly.
- **Customer Advocacy & Feedback:** Gather qualitative feedback from photographers and resort guests to directly influence our product engineering roadmap.

---

## Requirements
- 3+ years in customer success, account management, or hospitality operations (preferably within luxury resorts, theme parks, or attraction concessions).
- Outstanding verbal and written communication skills; comfortable presenting to senior resort executives and training front-line staff.
- High empathy, cultural adaptability, and enthusiasm for traveling to resort destinations internationally.
- Strong analytical skills; ability to interpret sales dashboards and deliver actionable revenue recommendations to venue partners.

---

## Nice-to-Haves
- Background in professional event or resort photography.
- Multilingual capabilities (e.g., Spanish, French, Indonesian/Bahasa, or Arabic).
- Experience with SaaS customer success platforms (Gainsight, HubSpot, Zendesk).
