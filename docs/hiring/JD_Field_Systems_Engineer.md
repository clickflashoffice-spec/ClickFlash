# Job Description: Field Deployment & Systems Engineer

**Company:** ClickFlash (Autonomous Photography & Resort Media Platform)
**Location:** Remote (with 40-60% travel to global resort sites)
**Compensation:** $120,000 - $150,000 base + Travel Per Diem
**Equity:** 1.0% - 1.5% (Pre-Seed, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
**Reporting To:** CTO / Technical Founder

## About ClickFlash
ClickFlash is an enterprise-grade autonomous photography concession and edge-to-cloud resort media operating system. We are transforming legacy resort photography with AI-driven culling, zero-friction biometric guest linking (ArcFace 512D), and dynamic yield pricing. Our ecosystem spans 17 distinct applications from edge Master nodes to guest WebXR galleries.

## The Role
As our first engineering hire alongside the CTO, you will be the linchpin for deploying our Master OS edge nodes into production at our initial pilot resorts (e.g., Bali, Maldives, Dubai). ClickFlash operates in a highly complex physical-meets-digital environment. You will be responsible for installing, configuring, and supporting the edge architecture on-site, integrating with Nikon cameras, thermal dye-sub printers, and local networks.

## What You'll Do (Responsibilities)
- **On-Site Deployment:** Travel to luxury resorts and theme parks to install and configure the ClickFlash Master OS (Electron/Fastify/SQLite) on edge hardware.
- **Hardware Integration:** Configure and troubleshoot Nikon DSLR camera tethering (USB/PTP/gphoto2) and DNP/Citizen thermal dye-sub printers.
- **Network & Systems Administration:** Design and configure resilient local network topologies (mDNS, Bonjour, LAN) for edge nodes ensuring 100% offline-first reliability.
- **Field Support & Debugging:** Act as the first line of technical support and escalation for resort operators and field photographers during pilot launches.
- **Feedback Loop:** Partner directly with the CTO to improve the deployment process, identify edge-case hardware bugs, and harden the system for scale.

## What We're Looking For (Requirements)
- 3+ years of experience in systems administration, edge computing, or field deployment engineering.
- Strong proficiency in Windows/Linux OS environments, scripting, and CLI tools.
- Experience with networking fundamentals (LAN, VPNs, mDNS, WebSockets).
- Hands-on experience troubleshooting physical hardware (cameras, printers, serial ports, or IoT devices).
- A high degree of autonomy, problem-solving under pressure, and the ability to work in remote physical environments.
- Excellent communication skills for training resort staff and IT teams.

## Nice-to-Haves
- Experience with Node.js, Fastify, or SQLite.
- Familiarity with Electron desktop applications.
- Exposure to BLE/UWB beacon technology.

## Why Join Us
This is a rare opportunity to join at the ground floor of an ambitious startup integrating cutting-edge computer vision, offline-first edge architecture, and consumer media. You will own the physical deployment of a highly sophisticated system and play a direct role in our path to Series A.
