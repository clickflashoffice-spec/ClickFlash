# Job Description: Sales & Partnerships Lead

**Company:** ClickFlash (Autonomous Photography & Resort Media Platform)
**Location:** Flexible (with 50%+ travel to global destinations)
**Compensation:** $100,000 - $130,000 base + Commission (OTE: $180,000 - $250,000)
**Equity:** 0.8% - 1.5% (Pre-Seed, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
**Reporting To:** CEO / Business Founder

## About ClickFlash
ClickFlash is an enterprise-grade autonomous photography concession and edge-to-cloud resort media operating system. We are transforming legacy resort photography with AI-driven culling, zero-friction biometric guest linking (ArcFace 512D), and dynamic yield pricing. We replace massive human editing teams and high-friction QR codes with an autonomous, AI-driven offline-first platform.

## The Role
As our first dedicated go-to-market hire, you will spearhead the commercialization of ClickFlash. We have enterprise-grade technology and pilot destinations ready (Bali, Maldives, Dubai), and we need a closer to turn early traction into a repeatable enterprise sales motion. You will sell to Resort GMs, Theme Park Operators, and Concession Directors.

## What You'll Do (Responsibilities)
- **Close Resort Pilots:** Convert our existing pipeline into signed, paying pilot deployments at luxury resorts and theme parks.
- **Enterprise Sales Motion:** Own the full sales cycle from prospecting to contract negotiation and closing.
- **Strategic Partnerships:** Build relationships with hotel groups, cruise operators, and tour companies for revenue-share agreements.
- **GTM Strategy:** Partner with the CEO to define pricing tiers, hardware-as-a-service packaging, and ROI models for venues.
- **Industry Presence:** Represent ClickFlash at major industry trade shows (IAAPA Expo, HITEC) and build a robust network in the attractions and hospitality space.

## What We're Looking For (Requirements)
- 5+ years of B2B enterprise sales experience, ideally in hospitality tech, travel tech, or SaaS.
- A proven track record of closing complex, high-ACV (Annual Contract Value) deals with multiple stakeholders.
- Existing network or deep understanding of the resort, theme park, or cruise line industries.
- Consultative selling approach: you can sell technical ROI (AI culling, dynamic yield) to non-technical buyers (Resort GMs).
- Willingness to travel 50%+ of the time to global resort destinations and trade shows.

## Nice-to-Haves
- Experience working at or selling against competitors like DEI (DigiPhoto), Pomvom, or similar concession operators.
- Understanding of payment gateways (Stripe) and international revenue splits.

## Why Join Us
This is a rare opportunity to be the founding sales leader for a product that has significant technical moats. The photography concession industry is ripe for disruption by AI, and you will have an unmatched product to sell, with a compensation package that aligns your success directly with the company's growth.
