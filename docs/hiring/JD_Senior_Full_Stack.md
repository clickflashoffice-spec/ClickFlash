# Job Description: Senior Full-Stack Engineer (React + Node)

**Company:** ClickFlash (Autonomous Photography & Resort Media Platform)
**Location:** Remote-First
**Compensation:** $150,000 - $180,000 base
**Equity:** 0.8% - 1.5% (Pre-Seed, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
**Reporting To:** CTO / Technical Founder

## About ClickFlash
ClickFlash is an enterprise-grade autonomous photography concession and edge-to-cloud resort media operating system. We are transforming legacy resort photography with AI-driven culling, zero-friction biometric guest linking (ArcFace 512D), and dynamic yield pricing. Our ecosystem spans 17 distinct applications from edge Master nodes to guest WebXR galleries.

## The Role
We are seeking an elite Senior Full-Stack Engineer to take ownership of our core web surfaces. You will partner directly with the CTO to drive feature velocity across our Management Command Hub, 3D WebXR Gallery, and Guest Self-Service PWA. This is a foundational hire that will shape our product as we deploy our first pilot resorts globally.

## What You'll Do (Responsibilities)
- **Frontend Architecture:** Own and scale our guest-facing and management SPA/PWAs using React 19, Vite, and Tailwind CSS v4.
- **Backend Systems:** Build and optimize fast, resilient backend services using Node.js (Fastify) and Cloudflare Workers (Hono/D1).
- **Payment Integration:** Implement complex Stripe Connect workflows for split disbursements, yield pricing models, and international checkout flows.
- **Performance & Polish:** Drive bundle splitting, Core Web Vitals optimizations, and deliver a frictionless "glassmorphic" UI/UX.
- **Real-Time Data:** Integrate WebSockets and WebRTC for live management dashboards and POV streams.

## What We're Looking For (Requirements)
- 5+ years of production experience as a Full-Stack Engineer.
- Deep expertise in React (Hooks, Suspense, Concurrent rendering), TypeScript, and modern state management (Zustand).
- Strong backend experience with Node.js, Fastify/Express, and SQL (SQLite/PostgreSQL).
- Proven track record of optimizing React application performance (bundle size, render cycles).
- Experience integrating complex payment APIs (Stripe).
- Eye for design and ability to implement pixel-perfect, responsive UI using Radix UI and Tailwind CSS.

## Nice-to-Haves
- Experience with Cloudflare Workers (Hono, D1, R2).
- Familiarity with 3D/WebXR (Three.js, Gaussian Splatting).
- Experience with WebRTC or live streaming architectures.
- Experience with offline-first PWAs and local-first databases (RxDB/Yjs).

## Why Join Us
You will be the second engineer in the door, taking ownership of critical paths in a ~300K LOC monorepo that is already operating at an enterprise scale. You will work on fascinating problems at the intersection of web tech, edge AI, and hardware, with massive upside in a rapidly growing, un-disrupted industry.
