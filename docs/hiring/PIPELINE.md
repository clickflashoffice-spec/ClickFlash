## 2. Interview Stage SLAs & Criteria

| Stage | Owner | Target Turnaround | Pass Threshold | Deliverable |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Resume / Profile Review** | Hiring Lead | $\le$ 24 hours | 7/10 Match on Core Stack | Decision to screen |
| **Initial Screen (20 min)** | Founder (CEO/CTO) | $\le$ 48 hours from apply | High energy, clear communicator, understands startup chaos | Invite to Technical Deep Dive |
| **Technical Deep Dive (45 min)** | CTO | $\le$ 72 hours from screen | Grade $\ge 4/5$ on architectural discussion | Issue paid practical challenge |
| **Practical Challenge (Paid $250)** | Candidate | 48-hour window | Meets Rubric Criteria (Clean code, handled edge cases) | Schedule Founder Final |
| **Founder Final (30 min)** | CEO + CTO | $\le$ 24 hours from submission | Culture match, compensation alignment | Verbal Offer within 24h |
| **Offer Extended** | CEO | $\le$ 24 hours from final | 5 business day offer acceptance window | Signed Offer Letter & IP Agreement |
