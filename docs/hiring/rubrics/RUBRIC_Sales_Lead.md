# Interview Rubric: Sales & Partnerships Lead (🟡 P1)

> **Role Focus:** Closing luxury resort concessions, waterparks, and cruise line media operators; displacing incumbent legacy providers (DEI, Pomvom, Fotiqo); negotiating dynamic rev-share and enterprise licensing models.

---

## 1. Evaluation Dimensions (Score 1 to 5)

| Dimension | Weight | Poor (1–2) | Target (3–4) | Exceptional (5) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Hospitality & Concession Domain Knowledge** | 30% | Generalist SaaS seller; unaware of how hotel GM incentives, concession splits, or photography contracts work. | Deep familiarity with resort operations, photography rev-shares ($15\%–30\%$), front-desk upsell friction, and seasonal occupancy cycles. | Possesses an immediate rolodex of resort General Managers, Directors of Operations, and concession executives in SE Asia, EMEA, or the Americas. |
| **Value Proposition & Competitive Displacement** | 25% | Relies on generic feature lists; cannot articulate why AI-first facial recognition beats legacy paper QR wristbands. | Convinces operators that zero-friction biometric delivery doubles capture rates and increases guest spend from $\$25$ to $\$65+$. | Seamlessly positions ClickFlash as a guest satisfaction driver (Net Promoter Score) while presenting airtight ROI and risk-free pilot models. |
| **Deal Structuring & Contract Negotiation** | 25% | Cedes ground immediately on pricing; uncomfortable with multi-tier enterprise contracts. | Structures pilot-to-contract terms (30-day paid pilot converting to 12–24 month exclusive concession). | Crafts innovative hybrid contracts combining minimum revenue guarantees, dynamic yield rev-shares, and annual software licensing fees. |
| **Pipeline Hygiene & Velocity** | 20% | Disorganized follow-ups; misses key decision-makers; passive waiting on inbound leads. | Rigorous CRM management; multi-threads accounts (GM, Finance Director, IT Lead); maintains predictable 60–90 day close cycles. | Ruthless qualification; builds high-converting outbound cadences tailored to resort peak planning seasons (Q2 for summer, Q4 for winter). |

## 2. Live Role-Play Assessment (30 Minutes)

### Scenario: "The Skeptical Luxury Resort General Manager"
- **Setting:** The candidate is pitching the General Manager of a 400-key five-star beach resort in Phuket. The resort currently has a 3-year contract with a legacy photography company that uses manual laminated tickets and high-pressure desk sales, generating modest revenue ($\approx \$15,000/\text{month}$) but frequent TripAdvisor guest complaints.
- **Candidate Objectives:**
  1. Uncover the GM's pain points around guest friction, desk queues, and brand perception.
  2. Differentiate ClickFlash's zero-friction biometric linking and private digital web gallery.
  3. Overcome objections regarding GDPR/biometric privacy concerns and upfront hardware costs.
  4. Secure agreement for a 30-day side-by-side or phased pilot launch for the upcoming high season.
- **Evaluation Criteria:**
  - Active listening and consultative questioning vs. steamrolling with slides.
  - Clarity on compliance (on-premise edge air-gapping, guest consent protocols).
  - Urgency creation and clear next steps agreed upon before closing the call.

## 3. Decision Matrix
- **Hire (Score $\ge 4.0/5$):** Combines executive presence with sharp commercial acumen; deeply understands resort hospitality dynamics.
- **No Hire (Score $< 3.5/5$):** Cannot move past standard transactional SaaS talking points; fails to grasp the physical concession business model.
