# ClickFlash Photographer Guide

Welcome to the team! This guide will teach you the standard operating procedure for taking photos, pairing guests with albums, and managing your equipment at the venue.

## 1. The RFID Workflow

ClickFlash uses RFID wristbands or cards to magically group photos for the guest.
1. **Greet the Guest**: Hand the guest an RFID wristband/card.
2. **Scan**: Tap the guest's RFID band on your scanner attachment or mobile device before the first photo of that group.
3. **Shoot**: Take as many photos as you want. All photos taken *after* that scan will automatically be dumped into that guest's digital album.
4. **Next Guest**: When you move to the next guest, just scan their new RFID band. The system will automatically cut off the previous album and start a new one.

## 2. Handling Missing Scans

If you forgot to scan a guest before shooting:
- Don't worry! Tell the guest their photos will be in the "Unclaimed" folder on the Touch Kiosk.
- They can use the kiosk's facial recognition feature to find their photos instantly.

## 3. Battery & Equipment Swaps

- When your camera or mobile scanner battery gets low, swap it out at the base station.
- Your photos are stored securely on the SD card (or transferred wirelessly if using the WiFi module). 
- If you are shooting with an SD card only, remember to drop the card off at the Master Hub PC every 2 hours so the operator can ingest the photos.

## 4. Helpful Tips for Better Sales

- **Take multiple angles**: Guests buy more when they have variety.
- **Remind them to visit the Kiosk**: Always end the interaction by saying, "You can view and buy these photos right now at the kiosks near the exit!"
- **Keep the RFID visible**: Make sure the RFID wristband is scanned cleanly; a successful scan usually beeps or flashes a green LED on your scanner.
